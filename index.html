Sources & proxy

Geo-blocked? Streams check the exit IP — public proxies are US/EU. For country-locked streams use Custom with your own worker/VPS in that country, or a VPN.
Why do channels fail?

Three usual causes: the link is dead (404), the source server is down (5xx), or the browser is blocked — CORS, mixed-content (http on an https page), hotlink protection, or geo-blocking (403). The player retries via proxy automatically and tells you which one it found.

Geo-blocked? (403 on everything)

Streams check your exit IP. Public proxies exit in the US/EU, so country-locked channels still fail. Fix: a VPN, or your own proxy in that country (server.js on a VPS there → Custom proxy here).

Buffering or slow?

Live sources vary by time of day. Try Next for another channel in the same category, or retry via proxy — sometimes the proxy route is faster.
